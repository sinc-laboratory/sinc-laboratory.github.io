Mark Cartwright, Magdalena Fuentes, and Sooyeon Lee receive new $800,000 NSF grant on accessible captioning on non-speech information. The grant, titled [Collaborative Research: HCC: Medium: AI-Supported Audio Captioning of Non-Speech Information](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/show-award?AWD_ID=2504643), will support 2 graduate students and a postdoc to research the design and development of AI-supported systems for captioning non-speech information in online media. Read more about the award [here](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/show-award?AWD_ID=2504643).
